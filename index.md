<div class="chapter__headline-clip">
<span class="chapter__headline">Wenbo (Vito) Zhu</span>
</div>
<div class="chapter__body">

**Founding AI Lead &amp; Head of AI Research** at [OpusClip](https://www.opusclip.com).
Joined when the team had ~5 engineers, pivoted, and now lead a **30+ cross-functional AI team** delivering next-generation multimodal &amp; generative video products.

I initiated, led, and shipped flagship systems including:
[OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/) · [ClipAnything](https://www.opus.pro/clipanything) · [Agent Opus](https://www.opus.pro/agent)

Before OpusClip: **Senior ML Engineer at ByteDance/TikTok** — founding engineer of [Gauthmath](https://www.gauthmath.com/) (100M+ downloads, world's first AI geometry solver).
Earlier: **Research Scientist at Cloudwalk Technology** — billion-scale face clustering engine deployed across 10+ cities (patented).

Both **OpusClip** and **Gauthmath** recognized by *Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)* as **Top 50 GenAI Apps** · **MSc (OR)** UC Berkeley · **BSc (IE &amp; Math)** Beihang University
